# -Taiwan-Life-Finance-and-Retirement-Simulator
Enter your age, occupation and salary, investment amount and daily expenses to find out your lifetime savings.
Input Section:

35 industries (selection will automatically calculate monthly salary, can be manually modified), number of months for year-end bonuses, annual salary adjustment, taxes and labor/health insurance, retirement savings goals.
Food (three meals a day), Clothing (seasonal, holiday clothes, shoes and glasses).
Housing: Renting, buying a house, or living with parents; preset rent and housing prices for 22 counties/cities (can be modified), as well as summer and non-summer electricity, water, and gas bills.
Transportation: Domestic, Japanese, European cars, and brands like Sanyang, Kymco, and Yamaha.
Family: 0 to 2 children, lavish or frugal upbringing (until age 22), cats, dogs, and fish.
Entertainment: Video games, overseas travel (11 locations can be selected multiple times).
Variable Factors: Price increases, NTD appreciation/depreciation, ETF returns and volatility, frivolous spending (subscriptions, miscellaneous expenses, greed for bargains).

Output Section:

How much money is left after death (today's currency value and account balance), and 10%–90% of the results from 200 random payouts. Range
Identity Tags: Debtor, Lazy Bum/Rats, Beggar, Poor, Ordinary, Moderately Well-off, Wealthy, Nouveau Riche; also attached is your 8-level wealth table showing your social class.
How much will you have left at retirement age 65? Compare it to your target, and use the 4% rule to calculate your monthly spending.
Net Worth Curve, marking the retirement point and target line.
Bar Chart of Total Lifetime Expenses.
Reasons for Success or Failure, automatically determined by factors such as savings rate, housing allocation, investment returns, accumulated frivolous spending, raising children, and buying a car.
